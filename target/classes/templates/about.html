// Our Story

FROM PASSION TO PREMIUM SERVICE

Veloce was born from a passion for automobiles and a gap in the market. Our founder, a lifelong petrolhead, noticed that truly exceptional cars were locked behind ownership — accessible only to a privileged few. We changed that.

Starting with just 5 cars in Jubilee Hills, we've grown to a 48-car fleet across 4 locations in Hyderabad, serving everyone from first-time supercar enthusiasts to seasoned collectors looking for a weekend thrill.
